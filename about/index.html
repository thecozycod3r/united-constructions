B K Harish founded United Constructions to bring a professional approach to construction in Bengaluru. Forty years later his son runs it, and the firm still works from Richmond Road.

UC—A1Our story

Remember building cities out of whatever was lying around the house as a child? That urge to make something that stands is still what drives us.

United Constructions was established in 1986 by Mr B K Harish, who saw how disorganised construction in the city was and set out to run it professionally. The first project, a flagship showroom for P C Mallappa & Co. on Infantry Road, was handed over in 1988.

Over the next four decades the firm built across Bengaluru's real-estate verticals: independent homes, apartment buildings, schools, an orphanage, commercial and co-working buildings, farmhouses and a resort. Construction is still the core. The firm is steadily becoming a one-stop team for engineering, design and interiors too.

Today Mr Prathik Harish leads the company. He has added an interiors division focused on detailed, well-made fittings and fixtures, so a client can go from foundation to furnished home with one team.

Most of our work comes from people who have built with us before, or from their friends. That is the measure we care about.
