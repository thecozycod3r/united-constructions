UC—PProcess

How a project runs, start to keys.

Seven stages. Each one ends with something you can see and sign off before the next one starts.

  1. Site visit

    We walk the plot or the existing building with you, look at soil, access, setbacks and neighbours, and listen to what you want it to become.

  2. Brief & feasibility

    We turn the conversation into a written brief: built-up area, rooms, budget band, timeline and what the plot will legally allow.

  3. Design

    We work with your architect, or ours, on plans, elevations and structure until the drawings answer the brief.

  4. Estimate & agreement

    You get a line-by-line estimate based on the drawings and a specification of materials and brands. Nothing starts until the scope is agreed in writing.

  5. Construction

    Our own site team runs the build in stages (foundation, structure, masonry, services, finishes) with a review at each stage.

  6. Interiors

    If you want them, our interiors team fits out the finished shell: joinery, ceilings, lighting and loose furniture.

  7. Handover

    A snag walk-through, then keys, drawings and warranties. We stay reachable after you move in.

UC—P2Before you call

What helps us help you.

  • Plot location and size, or the address of the building to renovate
  • Any drawings, even rough sketches or photos of homes you like
  • The number of floors and rooms you have in mind
  • A budget band, and when you would like to move in
