Privacy policy

How we handle the details you send us.

What we collect

When you contact us by phone, WhatsApp, email or the enquiry form, we receive the details you choose to share: your name, phone number, email address and information about your project.

How we use it

We use these details only to respond to your enquiry, plan and quote for your project, and keep you updated while we work together. We do not sell or rent your information.

Third-party services

The enquiry form opens WhatsApp or your email app, which handle the message under their own privacy policies. The map on our contact page is provided by Google Maps. Fonts are served by Google Fonts.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, email info@theunitedconstructions.com.
